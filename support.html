Support

Unbroken — Stoic Recovery

Contact

For questions, problems with the app, purchases, or requests to delete your data, email unbrokenapp.help@proton.me. We usually reply within a few days.

Purchases

Companion Access is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. If you reinstall the app or change phone, open the Companion and tap Restore purchases.

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (your Play Store order history).

If you are in crisis

Unbroken is not a crisis service. If you are thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number now, or find a crisis line in your country at findahelpline.com.
