Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Unbroken ("the app") is built by an individual developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account identifier. The app creates an anonymous account for you automatically — no email, name, or phone number is required to use the app.
  • Journal and reflection entries. Anything you write in the Journal, Today's Move, Relationship Review, or similar tools is saved so you can look back on it.
  • Companion conversations. Messages you send to the Companion, and its replies, are saved so the Companion can remember context across sessions.
  • Tracker data. No-contact streak counts, urge logs, and reset reasons you select.
  • Purchase status. If you purchase Companion Access, our payment provider (RevenueCat) and the relevant app store (Apple or Google) process your purchase. We receive confirmation of your purchase status, not your payment details.

What we don't collect

  • We do not require your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or location.

How your data is used

Your entries and conversations are used solely to operate the app for you: showing your history, tracking your streak, and giving the Companion the context it needs to respond helpfully. We do not use your personal entries to train AI models.

Third parties we use

  • Supabase — stores your data securely and handles anonymous authentication.
  • Anthropic — powers the Companion's AI responses. Your messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a reply; Anthropic's own privacy practices apply to that processing.
  • RevenueCat — manages purchase status for Companion Access.
  • ntfy.sh — delivers short notifications to the developer's phone during the early launch period (see below).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process payment if you purchase Companion Access. We never see your card details.

Safety and quality monitoring

If a conversation with the Companion suggests you may be in crisis, the app responds with safety information and encourages you to contact a crisis line or emergency services. The app is not a crisis service.

During the early launch period, the developer receives a short notification (via ntfy.sh) when a new Companion message is sent. It may include the first words of the message, so problems and safety issues can be spotted quickly. It does not include your name or any other information about you.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you use the app. To request deletion of your journal entries, chat history, or tracker data, contact the developer using the details below. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Children

This app is intended for users 18 and older, as stated in the in-app disclaimer. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your data, can be sent to unbrokenapp.help@proton.me.
